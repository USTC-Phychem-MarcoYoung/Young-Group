About Us

Young Group is a research team focused on theoretical and computational studies, with research areas intersecting physics, chemistry, and materials science.

We focus on the microscopic mechanisms and macroscopic emergent phenomena in condensed matter systems, molecular systems, and fluid systems, and are committed to research based on rigorous theoretical derivation and high-performance computational simulations.

Research Interests

Theoretical Physics and Chemistry

Develop theoretical models used to describe the quantum and statistical properties of physical and chemical systems.

Computational Condensed Matter Physics

Study the properties of materials, interfaces, phase transitions, and transport based on first principles.

Fluid Theory

Study of continuum mechanics, interfacial phenomena, transport processes, fluid stability, and nonequilibrium fluid phenomena.

Multiscale Simulation

Establish the connection between microscopic interactions and macroscopic observables.
