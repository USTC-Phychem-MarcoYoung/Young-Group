About Us

Young Group is a theoretical and computational research group working at the interface of physics, chemistry, and materials science.

Our research focuses on understanding microscopic mechanisms and emergent phenomena in condensed matter systems, molecular systems, and fluids through rigorous modeling and high-performance computation.

Research Interests

Theoretical Physics and Chemistry

We develop theoretical models for quantum, statistical, and thermodynamic properties of physical and chemical systems.

Computational Condensed Matter Physics

We use first-principles calculations, electronic-structure theory, and numerical simulations to investigate materials, interfaces, and phase behavior.

Fluid Theory

We study continuum theories, interfacial phenomena, transport processes, hydrodynamic stability, and non-equilibrium behavior in fluids.

Multiscale Modeling

We connect microscopic interactions with macroscopic observables through analytical theory, molecular simulation, and computational modeling.
